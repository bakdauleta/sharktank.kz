The Mission

We believe that the best managers are also great businesspeople. Shark Tank School Club is dedicated to de-mystifying complex financial theories and applying them to real-world school startups.

Core Competencies

Financial Modeling

Learn how to build cash flow statements, forecast revenue models, and analyze break-even points for any business idea.

Business Strategy

Dive deep into market research, SWOT analysis, and operational efficiency. Turn a concept into a viable plan.

Pitching & Negotiation

The art of the pitch is lost on too many students. Here we learn to communicate value, negotiate terms, and handle Q&A.

The Annual Challenge

Every year, we host the "Shark Tank" Final.


The Rules:

  • Students form teams (Max 3 people).
  • Develop a business plan based on your school's infrastructure.
  • 5 Minutes Pitching: Sell your vision.
  • Panel Q&A: Be grilled by the Sharks on financial viability.
